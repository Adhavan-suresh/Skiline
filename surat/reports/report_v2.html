Campaign Phases & Timeline
Jun 12–13, 2026 — Days 1–2
🟢 Learning Phase — Outstanding Performance
Campaign launched June 12. Meta's algorithm entered learning phase with immediate strong results: ₹15.43–₹15.49 CPL over 2 days, 11 leads from ₹170 spend. CTR of 3.4–4.3% confirmed strong creative-audience fit. These are among the best early CPL numbers observed across any Skiline city campaign. Audience was fresh and delivery was consistent.
Jun 14–15, 2026 — Days 3–4
🟡 Learning Phase Degradation
CPL rose to ₹47 on Jun 14 as Meta began exploring the broader audience (normal learning behavior). Jun 15 partial delivery (₹57 spend, 2 leads) — campaign hit its ad set end-date at midnight Jun 15 and stopped. This was an unintentional misconfiguration: the end-date was set to June 15, 2026 during setup. Delivery went to zero on Jun 16–17.
Jun 16–17, 2026 — Days 5–6
🔴 Zero Delivery — Accidental Expiry
Campaign end-date expired June 15. Zero spend, zero reach, zero leads for 2 days. Detected and fixed June 18 by setting end-date to December 31, 2026. However, the 2-day gap forced Meta to restart the learning phase from scratch — completely resetting the algorithm's optimization.
Jun 18, 2026 — Day 7
🔴 Learning Phase Reset — ₹158 CPL
On restart, Meta re-entered the full learning phase. Only 1 lead generated on ₹158 spend — worst CPL of the entire campaign. This is standard Meta behavior: after any significant interruption (>2 days), the algorithm treats the campaign as new and must re-explore the audience. The ₹15 CPL performance from Days 1–2 was effectively lost.
Jun 19–21, 2026 — Days 8–10
🟡 Recovery Phase
Algorithm recovered steadily: Jun 19 = ₹32.59 CPL (5 leads), Jun 20 = ₹27.45 CPL (6 leads), Jun 21 = ₹23.28 CPL (6 leads). Clear downward CPL trend confirmed Meta was optimizing again. Jun 21 was the best CPL post-restart at ₹23.28 with the highest CTR of the recovery period at 6.51%.
Jun 22–26, 2026 — Days 11–15
🟡 Declining Efficiency — CTR & Volume Drop
Performance deteriorated from Jun 22 onward: CTR dropped from 6.5% to 2.4–3.0%, CPL climbed to ₹39–₹118. Jun 23 produced 0 leads on ₹99 spend. Jun 25 = ₹118 CPL (1 lead). Likely causes: (1) audience fatigue in small Surat pool, (2) frequency approaching saturation at 1.5× average, (3) Meta beginning to exhaust high-intent users. Campaign paused Jun 26.

⚠ Geographic Targeting Issue — Root Cause Analysis
Sales team finding: Multiple leads reported from locations outside Surat — some as far as 300km away. This has been confirmed as a real targeting configuration issue, not a data anomaly.
Root Cause: Location Type set to "home + recent"

The ad set was configured with location_types: ["home", "recent"]. The "recent" type tells Meta to show ads to anyone who has been physically present in the Surat + 30km zone in the last 30 days — regardless of where they currently live or work.

This means a professional from Delhi, Rajasthan, or Andhra Pradesh who visited Surat for a business trip or family function in the last month would have seen and submitted the lead form — even though they are now back home 300–500km away.

Recommendations for Phase 2
1. Fix location targeting (critical — do before relaunch)
Change location_types from ["home","recent"] to ["home"] only. This stops out-of-city leads immediately. One API call change — no creative or budget change needed.
2. Increase budget to ₹200–250/day minimum
At ₹100/day with ₹30–35 CPL, the campaign gets only 3 leads/day max. Meta needs ~50 leads in 7 days to exit learning. ₹200/day = 5–6 leads/day = exits learning in ~9 days. This is what reduced Chennai CPL from ₹30+ to ₹18.
3. Manual placement selection
Exclude: Audience Network, In-stream Video, Marketplace, Desktop. Keep: Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook Reels, Stories. Estimated 8–12% CPL improvement from eliminating zero-lead placements.
4. Narrow to males only or create separate male/female ad sets
Female segments produced only 3 leads (7.5%) on ₹187 spend (13.2% of budget). Either go male-only to improve efficiency, or create a separate smaller female ad set with different creative.
5. New creative to fight audience fatigue
CTR dropped from 6.5% (Jun 21) to 2.4% (Jun 22–23) in a single day. Surat audience pool is small — existing users have likely seen the ad 1.5× already. Launch with a fresh creative on relaunch. Video/Reel format may outperform static image in round 2.
6. Set end-date to Dec 31, 2026 and verify before launch
The accidental expiry cost ₹400+ and reset learning. Always verify end-date is set to end of year before launching any campaign. Consider also scheduling a monitoring check at 7 days.
